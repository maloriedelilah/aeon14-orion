Simply put, I've always wanted to be a writer. I began my first story (I still have a copy of it, dear stars, it's terrible) when I was eleven years old. I'd just finished reading Lord of the Rings and didn't want the story to end, so I created my own world (Ilendia) and filled it with amazing creatures and their tales. I continued penning fantasy stories, and even a Buffy the Vampire Slayer season 8 fan-fic novel (which was the first full-length novel I ever finished) before I watched the movie Serenity and knew I had to write a story like that! With that revelation, Aeon 14 was born. Over the years I've had a lot of jobs. I've run an ISP, game server company, worked in the corporate world for some very big names running some very big systems and products. I've been a solo developer, a team leader, even a CTO & CIO. What should be a two-car garage attached to our house is a rather well-equipped woodworking shop, and I've built a greenhouse so I can grow plants year round. Suffice to say, I'm a maker. Creating things is what I do. But of all those things, Aeon 14 is by far my greatest accomplishment, and I'm excited to be able to share it with you.
